YOUR BROWSER. YOUR INTEGRATION.

A small tool.
A direct connection.

Requests go directly from the extension to Adobe. There’s no companion server to run and no developer-operated backend receiving your program data.

You provide your own authorized Adobe integration. Saving a credential is optional. Saved secrets use a bundled encryption key; this is obfuscation, not a secure vault. Check your organization’s requirements before choosing that option.

INSTALLATION

From the Store
to your toolbar.

Install through the official Chrome Web Store listing.

Add to Chrome ↗
  1. 01

    Add it from the Chrome Web Store.

    Open the Chrome Web Store listing in Chrome and choose Add to Chrome. Review the requested permissions and confirm installation.

  2. 02

    Keep it in your toolbar.

    Open Chrome’s Extensions menu and pin AEM Cloud Manager Companion for quick access.

  3. 03

    Connect your program.

    Open Settings. Enter your Adobe Client ID, Organization ID, Program ID, Client Secret and scopes, then choose Connect. You can also use a manually supplied token.
